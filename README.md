# CareSync
**AI-Powered Critical Patient Monitoring & Response Intelligence System**

Built for NEURALZENATHON 2026.

## Problem
In hospital general wards, nurses monitor vitals manually at intervals. A patient who
looks stable can deteriorate suddenly (a drop in SpO₂, abnormal heart rate, blood
pressure or temperature), and the delay in noticing it can mean a late ICU transfer
and worse outcomes.

## Solution
CareSync combines a low-cost, adhesive wearable patch with a hospital server and
dashboard:

- **Continuous monitoring:** the patch tracks essential vitals and streams them in real time.
- **AI risk assessment:** abnormalities and deterioration risk are detected from live data.
- **Dynamic prioritization:** patients are ranked by criticality, with the highest-risk cases at the top.
- **Intelligent alerts:** critical events notify the responsible staff, who must acknowledge them.
- **Role-based access:** separate logins for doctors, nurses and administrators, each seeing their assigned patients.
- **Interactive dashboard:** ward heatmap, patient deep-dive views and hospital-wide analytics.
